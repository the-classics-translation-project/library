# Glossary — *The War of the Worlds*, Japanese draft

All entries below are **provisional translation choices**, not approved terminology. They reflect the supplied Japanese guide where applicable. The first table records forms actually used in translated node `n0005`; the second retains useful work-wide suggestions for continuity without implying those passages have been translated in this task.
