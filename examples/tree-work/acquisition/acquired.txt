The Last Ferry

Part One

The river was quiet that evening.

The quay

At dusk Mara reached the quay with a letter she had promised not to read. The ferryman was called Leno. He looked once at the letter, and then at the river.

“There's still time to go back,” he said.

Mara put one foot into the boat. Behind her, a bell sounded from the town. A shuttered café across the street bore a peeling blue ship sticker: 🚢.

On the far bank, one window was already lit.

The crossing

Leno steered into the mist. Mara asked whether the far bank had a name.

“Every place has a name,” he said. “Not every traveler needs to know it.”

The letter remained unopened in her pocket. The boat struck something in the dark and continued on.

When Mara looked back, the town had vanished.
