By the time Mara reached the quay, the light was fading. In her pocket was a letter she had promised not to open.

The ferryman, Leno, glanced at the letter and then at the river. “There's still time to go back,” he said.

Mara stepped into the boat. A bell rang behind her, somewhere in the town. Across the street, the shuttered café still had a blue ship sticker—🚢—peeling from its window.

On the opposite bank, a single window glowed in the dark.
